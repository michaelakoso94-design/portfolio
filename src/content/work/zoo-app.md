## The problem

Parents want an experience that is fun and also teaches their kids something. The zoo couldn't answer a child's questions anymore, because the signs were neglected. Popular attractions were full, and the petting area was hard to reach.

## Research

I worked with personas and usability scripts, then mapped opportunities, goals and pain points. An Octalysis analysis helped me decide which motivations a gamified layer should speak to, and a customer journey map showed where the visit breaks down.

## Design

From IA and user flows to the final screens: splash, onboarding, avatar editing, the map and the wallet.
